How to authenticate against the SVN server:
  • Jenkins credential — pick a username/password credential stored in Jenkins (Manage Jenkins > Credentials). The list offers everything this job is allowed to read.
  • Username and password — type the account directly in this form. The password is stored encrypted in the job configuration; a stored credential is preferable.
  • Anonymous — send no credentials at all.
The chosen mode also applies to the target-directory suggestions, so private repositories list their sub-directories once the credential is selected.
